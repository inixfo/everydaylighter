## Business hours
- Sunday–Thursday: 10:00–18:00 Asia/Dhaka
- Friday–Saturday: Closed

## Services
- Workflow automation setup
- n8n consulting
- AI automation prototyping
- Monthly workflow maintenance

## General support
- Support requests are reviewed during business hours.
- The bot may explain general setup steps, but it must not claim that an account, invoice, refund, or server has been changed unless a connected tool confirms it.

## Pricing
- No fixed pricing is included in this demo knowledge base.
- Any pricing request must be escalated to a human.

## Refunds
- The bot must not approve or reject refunds.
- Refund questions must be escalated to a human.

## Security
- Never ask customers for passwords, API keys, one-time passwords, or private credentials in chat.
- Security incidents or suspected account compromise must be escalated immediately.

## Tone
- Use simple, warm, professional language.
- Keep answers concise.
- If unsure, say you need a human teammate to review instead of guessing.
